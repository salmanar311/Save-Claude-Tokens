02

The Problem Most People Have

There are two independent limits — and most people don't even know which one they're hitting.

5-Hour Limit

Rolling Window

Resets every 5 hours. This is what most people hit during deep work sessions.

7-Day Limit

Weekly Cap

Resets every 7 days. Harder to reach, but regular file uploads will get you there.

Every new message you send makes Claude re-read the entire conversation from the beginning. So a long chat gets progressively more expensive per reply — even if your question is one line. Most people are wasting 60–80% of their tokens without realising it.

04

Shortcut Codes That Save Tokens

Add one of these before your prompt to change how Claude responds — shorter output, sharper answers, less waste.

BLUNT:Skip the caveats and disclaimers. Just give me the answer.
BRUTAL:No softening. Tell me exactly what's wrong with this.
COUNTER:What's the strongest argument against my plan?
STEELMAN:Give me the best possible version of the opposing argument.
LADDER:Explain it at three levels: beginner, intermediate, expert.
2ND_FX:What are the second-order effects of this decision?
In a 1-on-1 session we'll build a shortcut set that's specific to your work — not a generic list, but codes tuned to what you actually do every day.
